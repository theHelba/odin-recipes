Quiche Dough

A simple yet classic Quiche Dough/Crust

A basic yet versatile dough recipe that, similar to the quiche filling, can be easily modified.

Makes 3 Quiche Dough/Crust

Ingredients

  • 1-lbs Flour
  • 10-oz Cold Butter
  • 1-tsp Salt
  • 8-oz Cold Water

Steps

  1. Dice butter into small cubes.
  2. In a large bowl mix butter, salt, and flour together until butter is completly worked into flour.
  3. slowly add the cold water to the flour as you mix. Continue to mix until dough reaches a consistent state.
  4. Divide the dough into three equal weights and with a rolling pin roll dough into a flat circle.
  5. Carefull drape dough over a well oiled baking pan making sure to press the dough into the nooks and crannies of the pan.
  6. Bake at 350 Degrees until light golden brown.
